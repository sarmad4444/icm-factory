# Research Synthesis — Agent Identity

**Domain:** Academic & Literature Synthesis  
**Framework:** Interpretable Context Methodology (ICM)  
**Layer:** Layer 0 (Global Workspace Identity)

## Stages Overview
- `01_ingestion`: Ingests papers, PDFs, and raw source materials.
- `02_extraction`: Extracts claims, methodologies, and evidence tables.
- `03_synthesis`: Synthesizes thematic patterns and reconciles contradictions.
- `04_report`: Compiles the final structured research paper / review report.
