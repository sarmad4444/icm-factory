# Stage 02: Tradeoff Analysis — Contract

**Stage:** `stages/02_tradeoffs`  
**Purpose:** Explore architectural options and evaluate tradeoffs.

---

## Inputs

- `stages/01_discovery/output/problem_statement.md`
- `resources/rules.md`

---

## Process

1. Identify at least 2 architectural candidate solutions (e.g. Option A vs Option B).
2. Compare them across performance, operational complexity, cost, and developer velocity.
3. Formulate a preliminary recommendation with clear rationale.

## Outputs

- `stages/02_tradeoffs/output/tradeoff_matrix.md`
